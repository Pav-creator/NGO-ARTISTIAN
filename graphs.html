(2) Key findings

Skill. 52.0% require operational assistance. The English barrier (29.8%) acts as the primary roadblock. Budget. While 31.7% have flexible budgets, 24.0% rely strictly on free tools, highlighting the need for low-cost, open-source tech stacks. Trust. Verification badges (35.6%) and transparent artisan stories build the highest consumer confidence.
