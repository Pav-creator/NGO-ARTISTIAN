(1) Survey Data Visualisation (n=104)

The visualizations below are interactive. Hover or tap on the graph slices to view detailed percentages. Overall, 75.9% believe a website would enhance their reach.

Q1. Age Demographics
18–25 (50.0%)
Under 18 (20.2%)
Above 25 (15.4%)
Unspecified (14.4%)
Half of the respondents belong to the 18–25 age bracket. This highlights that a younger demographic of local creators is actively exploring digital transitions.
Q2. Would a website help?
Yes, absolutely (44.2%)
Maybe (31.7%)
Not sure (20.2%)
Offline better (3.8%)
A strong majority (75.9% combined) acknowledge that a dedicated website would significantly boost their reach. Only a negligible 3.8% strongly prefer offline.
Q3. Current Marketing Reach
Word of mouth (33.7%)
Social media (29.8%)
Exhibitions (26.0%)
No reach (10.6%)
Traditional Word of Mouth (33.7%) remains the dominant strategy. Alarmingly, over 10% have zero reach outside their local vicinity, a critical growth bottleneck.
Q4. Training required?
A little guidance (33.7%)
Self-learn (27.9%)
No need (20.2%)
Full training (18.3%)
More than half of the artisans (52% combined) require technical guidance or full training to manage a website, indicating a strong need for educational support.
Q5. Preferred Site Feature
NGO story (31.7%)
Payments (28.8%)
Contact form (26.0%)
Gallery (13.5%)
Brand authenticity through the NGO/Artisan Story (31.7%) is highly demanded, closely followed by payments. Users want connection before transactions.
Q6. Biggest Digital Barrier
English UI (29.8%)
Fraud fear (24.0%)
Slow internet (24.0%)
Order tracking (22.1%)
The English UI barrier (29.8%) is the primary hurdle. Fear of online fraud and slow connectivity also hold back nearly half the respondents from digital expansion.
Q7. Preferred Contact Method
WhatsApp/Phone (33.7%)
Social media (27.9%)
Website form (20.2%)
Email (18.3%)
Instant messaging platforms like WhatsApp (33.7%) are the most comfortable channels. Websites should integrate direct chat links rather than complex forms.
Q8. Monthly Digital Budget
Above ₹5,000 (31.7%)
₹1k–₹5k (26.0%)
Free tools only (24.0%)
Depends (18.3%)
While 31.7% can invest over ₹5,000, a significant 24.0% strictly seek zero-cost tools, highlighting the necessity for heavily subsidized static tech solutions.
Q9. Expected Dev Support
Bug fixes (29.8%)
Training (24.0%)
Maintenance (24.0%)
No support (22.1%)
Developers must provide continued assistance. Bug fixing (29.8%) and regular maintenance (24.0%) prove development isn't just a one-time handover.
Q10. Primary Trust Factor
Security badges (35.6%)
Reviews (22.1%)
Artisan stories (22.1%)
Return policy (20.2%)
Visible security verification badges (35.6%) act as the strongest catalyst for consumer trust. Clear display of authentic customer reviews follows closely behind.
