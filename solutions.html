Solutions & Recommendations

Actionable strategies to bridge the digital divide for local creators based on field research findings.

How to Fix the Problems?

✓ 1. Simplify Website Design & Local Language Support

Web development for NGOs should focus on minimalist interfaces with local language (Hindi/Marathi) toggles. Complex e-commerce checkout flows should be replaced with simple "Inquire via WhatsApp" buttons to reduce friction and technical burden.

✓ 2. Implement Trust-Building Features

To counter the fear of online fraud, websites must include a detailed "Our Story" section outlining the artisan's journey. Displaying customer reviews, clear return policies, and "Verified NGO/Creator" badges are essential to build consumer confidence.

✓ 3. Cost-Effective & Open-Source Tools

To address the budget constraints (₹1,000 - ₹5,000), agencies should utilize open-source platforms, static site generators (hosted for free on platforms like Vercel or GitHub Pages), and subsidized hosting schemes to keep monthly maintenance costs near zero.

✓ 4. Conduct Digital Literacy & Training Camps

Before handing over a website, structured training modules must be provided. These hands-on workshops should teach creators basic product uploading, safe digital communication, and how to track online inquiries effectively.
