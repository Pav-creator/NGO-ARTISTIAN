Website Requirement Study for NGOs & Artisans

A field research project exploring the digital awareness, marketing strategies, and technical barriers faced by local creators and social organizations in India.

About NGOs & Traditional Artisans

Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) and traditional artisans form the backbone of the grassroots economy and cultural heritage. They provide essential community services, handmade crafts, handloom textiles, and sustainable products.


However, despite the rapid growth of e-commerce in India, many of these creators still rely on fragmented offline marketing like word-of-mouth (33.3%) or local exhibitions. Establishing a dedicated digital presence is crucial for them to expand their market reach beyond local boundaries, tell their authentic stories, and connect directly with supporters.

Key Problems Identified in Survey Data

1. Technical & Language Barriers

Survey results indicate that the English language interface and complex web management systems are major hurdles. Over 46% of respondents require guided training just to operate basic digital tools.

2. Fear of Online Fraud & Trust Issues

Both creators and their customers face a severe lack of trust in digital transactions. "Fear of online fraud" was cited as a major difficulty, making NGOs hesitant to integrate online payments.

3. Severe Budget Constraints

Website development and maintenance are perceived as expensive. About 31.1% of surveyed artisans want completely free tools, while another 28.9% can only afford a minimal budget (₹1,000 - ₹5,000 monthly).

4. Lack of Post-Launch Support

Creators fear abandonment after a website is built. They struggle with technical bugs and content updates, highlighting a critical need for ongoing, simplified maintenance support.
