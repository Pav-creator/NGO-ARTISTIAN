Local Craft. Real Reach.

Home · Data Collection · Graphs & Results · About

(1) Method

Descriptive field study, quantitative and qualitative. Population: NGOs, artisans, local creators — crafts, handloom, NGO services, household goods. Sampling: random / stratified random, n = 104. Tools: Google Forms, interview schedule, observation notes. Analysis in Excel: percentages, frequency, charts; interviews grouped by theme.

Problem statement: “To understand the website requirements, digital readiness, and challenges faced by NGOs and artisans, and to suggest training modules and feature-rich website development to increase their online visibility.” Ethics: voluntary, verbal consent, confidential academic use. Limits: busy schedules, translation needs, connectivity gaps, selected locations.

(2) Survey Questionnaire

The following structured questions were presented to our 104 respondents:

1. What is your age?
  • Less than 18
  • Between 18-25
  • Above 25
  • Prefer not to say
2. Do you think having a dedicated website would help your products reach a wider audience?
  • Yes, absolutely
  • Maybe it would help
  • Not sure
  • No, offline is better
3. How do you currently showcase or market your products to potential buyers outside your local area?
  • Word of mouth and referrals
  • Exhibitions, fairs, and local events
  • Social media posts and status updates
  • We have no reach outside our local area
4. Do you or your team members need any training or guidance to operate a website?
  • Yes, full training is needed
  • A little guidance will work
  • We can figure it out ourselves
  • No need at all
5. If a website is built for you, what would you want to see on it first?
  • Product photo gallery and prices
  • Story and details about your NGO/work
  • Direct customer contact or order option
  • Online payment integration
6. What do you find most difficult about using digital tools?
  • Language barrier (English)
  • Managing and tracking orders
  • Fear of online fraud
  • Slow internet connectivity
7. What do customers find easiest for connecting with you?
  • Phone call or WhatsApp chat
  • Social media messages
  • Direct website inquiry form
  • Email
8. How much can you spend monthly to grow your business or NGO online?
  • A little (₹1,000 - ₹5,000)
  • Nothing (want free tools)
  • More than ₹5,000
  • Depends on project results
9. What kind of support do you expect from a web developer or designer after the website is launched?
  • Regular maintenance and updates
  • Training sessions for managing content
  • Fixing technical bugs when they occur
  • No ongoing support needed
10. Which feature is most important for building trust with your customers on the website?
  • Customer reviews and testimonials
  • Verified security badges and certificates
  • Clear return and refund policies
  • Detailed about us and artisan stories
