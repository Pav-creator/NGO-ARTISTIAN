(1) Methodology

Descriptive field study utilizing both quantitative and qualitative approaches.

Population: Local NGOs, traditional artisans, and community creators in Mumbai (Malad, Kandivali, etc.).
Sampling: Random stratified sampling, n = 104.
Tools: Google Forms (primary survey), unstructured interview schedules, and field observation notes.
