(1) Conclusion & Recommendations

Awareness is growing; however, digital adoption remains partial. Most respondents understand the value of dedicated websites, yet many still rely heavily on offline word-of-mouth supplemented by occasional social media usage.

Key Recommendations: Host digital awareness camps; develop local-language and low-bandwidth interfaces; provide hands-on CMS training; establish subsidized hosting tiers; and run consumer security education campaigns.
