(1) Conclusion & recommendations

Awareness is growing; adoption is partial. Most know websites help, yet many still rely on offline word-of-mouth plus occasional social media. Barriers: devices, connectivity, fraud fear, English UI, no training, tight budgets. Attitude to learning is positive with proper guidance.

Recommendations: awareness camps; local-language, low-internet-friendly interfaces; hands-on training; better connectivity and helplines; security education; subsidised hosting; collaboration between government, developers and associations; continuous monitoring; digital literacy; customer campaigns.
